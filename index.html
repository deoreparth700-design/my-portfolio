ABOUT ME

Engineering Philosophy & Background

I am a final-year Computer Science Engineering student at School of Engineering & Technology, MGM University. My primary technical focus is robust backend engineering: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Redis at the core, paired with defensive security and containerization.

My philosophy is "Contract before Code": I design data models, state flows, and API specifications before writing a single endpoint. Prior to implementing complex features, I construct formal architecture artifacts — Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), UML Use Case models, and Entity-Relationship schemas — ensuring systems are predictable, resilient, and explainable.

I pursue a structured self-directed study regimen of 1–2 hours every day, exploring distributed architectures, transactional guarantees, and concurrency. I am actively looking for a backend or full-stack placement or internship where this disciplined technical foundation creates tangible impact for real users.
